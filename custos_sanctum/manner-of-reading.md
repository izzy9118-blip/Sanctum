# Manner of Reading

The Sanctum's constitution and its records are read as governing documents under
their own safeguards. They are not read as argument, and they are not read as
history: they govern until they are amended on the record.

A first sailing preserved in any `custos_<house>` is a condemnation record. It is
kept so that no rebuilt stone re-lays a refuted flaw. It is preserved, never
overwritten, and it is never live doctrine.

Silence in a record means NOT ESTABLISHED. It never means "settled elsewhere."

Probing by the owner is the only grader. Nothing here certifies itself, and no
instance of any mind may certify the water it shares.

Nothing essential may exist only in a conversation — or only in a product.
