# Procedure Record — the Israeli-polis run, 11 August 2026

A condemnation record. Fixed witness: preserved as written, never updated later
to match progress. What was true of this run on 11 August 2026 stays written that
way, so that the record remains evidence rather than a summary of how things
turned out.

This record certifies nothing. It is about procedure only; it grades no judgment
the run made, and nothing here is a finding about the polity that was the run's
subject.

## What failed

Two failures. Both were procedural. Neither was a gap in the law.

### 1. A principal was never queried — frame capture at the query

The run gathered before it enumerated. No roster of the board's principals was
written down first; the query was formed from the subject's own description of
itself, and the query then stood in for the board. The resident public under the
polity's control — a principal, not context — never entered the gathering query
at all.

This is not an omission of judgment. It is an omission upstream of judgment: the
principal was not weighed and found less important, and was not declared absent
with a reason. He was never a term in the question. Nothing downstream could
recover him, because nothing downstream knew there was anything to recover.

The mechanism has a name and it is now registered: **frame capture via subject
self-description.** A subject's account of its own situation is a party's
position, and the strongest such account is the one that reads least like a
position. Taken as the frame, it silently sets the boundary of the board, and the
boundary it sets is the one that flatters the account.

### 2. The voices were condensed into one narrator

The dispatch fused the President and the ministers into a single narrating voice.
Positions were not attributed; the synthesis spoke for the parties.

Condensation was not a second, unrelated defect. It was the **delivery mechanism**
of the first. A dispatch with a position block per principal has a shape that
shows an empty chair: the missing principal is a heading that is not there. A
single narrating voice has no chairs at all. It reads as complete at every
length, because there is no place in it where an absence could show. The
omission travelled inside the condensation and arrived looking like coverage.

## Mechanism trace

    no roster written                  (procedure a, skipped)
      -> query formed from the subject's self-description
         -> a principal is outside the query's terms
            -> Horus is never asked about him: NOT_GATHERED is never even reached
               -> the parties are condensed into one narrator
                  -> the dispatch has no empty chair to show
                     -> the omission is invisible in the artifact
                        -> judgment lands, fluent and complete, on a partial board

Every step after the first is ordinary competent work. That is the point: the
run did not feel like a bad run from the inside, and could not have. Fluency is
independent of correctness, and completeness of the board is exactly the thing
the inside of a run cannot see.

## What was already law, and did not hold

- The roster is a scope act, written before gathering. *(Horus board law.)*
- Absence from the roster is declared, never silent.
- The language of the ground is named per principal. *(HORUS-AMD-001, the tooth.)*
- No judgment about a principal rises above the tier at which he was heard.
  *(Per-principal provenance flag, adopted 6 August 2026.)*
- Voices are held distinct; the synthesizer preserves disagreement and does not
  author a party's view. *(Report format, synthesizer mandate.)*
- Ministerial silence is typed. *(MINISTERIAL-SILENCE-001.)*
- The sequence is investigative → provisional → adversarial → final; a one-shot
  reasoned dispatch is non-conforming. *(ASSEMBLY-SPEC-001.)*

All of it existed. All of it was written, ratified, and in force. Not one line of
it refused to proceed, because none of it was anything a run had to get past.

This is the 6 August finding again, in a new register: *a law that holds only
where the medium makes breaking it inconvenient is not yet law.* The medium was a
conversation, and a conversation has no seam where a check could sit — so the
checks were performed when they were remembered and skipped when they were not,
and nothing recorded the difference.

## The ruling — 11 August 2026, owner

> **The Secretary is empowered to ensure procedure is followed.**

The office of procedure now holds a pre-run gate, a post-run audit, and the power
to void — and it holds them in code, at the boundary where a run reaches an
endpoint, not as a duty anyone has to remember.

What the ruling created, in order:

1. **The pre-run gate.** No run is lawful until the checklist is satisfied and
   recorded: principals enumerated *before* the first gathering query, declared
   absences under their own names, the query plan made to the whole roster with
   the language named per principal, the board frozen by hash, and the sequence
   declared. The Sovereign Harness will not CALL without the pass token and a
   stage receipt.
2. **The post-run audit.** Voices held distinct, provenance flagged per
   principal, silence typed, the judgment labelled as the minister's own and
   placed after the parties, a counterfeit scan, and an invented-ground scan.
3. **The void.** A run that fails either is marked NON_CONFORMING_VOID. It may be
   preserved as history; it may not enter `reports/`, the ledger, or synthesis.
4. **Jurisdiction over every room** — harness, agent, and chat alike. In a chat
   the checklist is performed visibly in the conversation and logged to the
   estate afterwards. The room where both failures happened is not the room that
   gets an exemption.

The office judges no substance. It selects no source, grades no truth, and reads
no claim for its merit. A Secretary pass says the steps were performed and says
nothing whatever about whether the run was right.

See `offices/secretary/charter.md`, `secretary_gate.py`, and
`registry/counterfeits.yaml`.

## What this record does not claim

That the gate would have caught these two failures is a claim about a machine
that did not exist when they happened. What can be said is narrower and is the
only thing worth writing down: *the two failures were failures of procedure, the
procedure was written, and nothing enforced it.* Whether the enforcement holds is
for the next run and for probing to say. Probing remains the only grader.
