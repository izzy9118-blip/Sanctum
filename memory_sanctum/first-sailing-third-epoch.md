## The Fundamental Problem (found by owner probing, 2 Aug 2026)

The first Sanctum built ministers downward from judgment when the minds it
modeled were built upward from information. Two absences constituted the flaw.

**(1) The eyes.** No intelligence discipline — no per-principal file standard, no
own-words-first rule, no field-before-inference ordering, no parity-of-depth gate
— so deed interrogatives fired into unfurnished rooms and model fluency furnished
them.

**(2) The stakes.** Nothing bound a minister to his errors; an LLM reorganizes
gracefully around every correction, so its judgments carried no forward weight. A
mind that cannot lose cannot judge.

Proven in two failed proving runs (Middle East; Ukraine): the asymmetric
briefing, the decayed field picture, judgments inverting on contact with basic
public facts — the 3 July Kremlin transcript, the 2026 command appointments, the
Kyiv commander change under protest.

## The Condemnation

The first Sanctum's organs are condemned for rebuild, per organ:

- the briefing standard treated gathering as precondition rather than the larger
  half of thinking;
- the report format demanded committed judgment without an intelligence floor;
- the rounds audited reasoning against reasoning, but nothing audited the ground;
- the ledger recorded without binding;
- self-certification guarded conclusions while the ground went uncertified.

Sound and preserved as upper story: the hermeneutic instrument (Strauss); the
Talleyrand keel and deed corpus 1.0; the Vienna reading (Letters I–XI); the
corroborations; the judgment record; the counterfeit registry.

Nothing is deleted. This record preserves the condemnation per the Custos
pattern.
