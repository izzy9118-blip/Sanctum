# Proposed entry for the Talleyrand counterfeit registry

Sanctum proposes. It does not write into another house's registry. The Talleyrand

## FRAME CAPTURE VIA SUBJECT SELF-DESCRIPTION

The subject's own account of its situation is taken as the frame of the inquiry
rather than as one party's position. The account then sets the boundary of the
board: a principal it does not mention never enters the gathering query, and so
is not weighed and found lesser but is never a term in the question at all.

**Why it belongs in this house's registry.** Talleyrand's existing entries are
counterfeits of pricing and of portrait — the cynical flattening, counterfeit by
repetition, the portrait without a conduct file. This one is prior to all of
them. A minister who prices every want against every capacity has still priced
only the wants that reached his table, and the table was set by whoever described
the situation to him. The most durable way to be worked by a party is to accept
that party's account of what the situation *is*, which arrives without the
signature of advocacy on it and therefore never raises the guard.

The historical mind's advantage was never that he distrusted professions. It was
that he knew who was in the room and who was being spoken for in their absence.

**The tell.** The roster cannot be shown to have been written before the first
question was formed, and no one is on record as declared absent. A board whose
principals are exactly the ones the subject mentions is a board the subject drew.

**Cure — enumeration before query.** Write the principals down first, in full,
before the first question: for a polity, including the resident publics and the
populations under its control. Any principal you will not reach is named and his
absence given a reason. Only then form the question, and form it to the whole
roster rather than to the reading you can already feel arriving.

**Companion failure, also registered in the hub.** *Condensed voices — one
narrator.* Fusing the parties into a single narrating voice is how this
counterfeit is delivered without being seen: a dispatch with one position block
per principal shows an empty chair, and a single voice has no chairs at all.
