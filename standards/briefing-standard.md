# Sanctum Briefing Standard

Governs how current fact enters a minister's context.

## Principle

Bias enters disguised as the given — and the briefing is the given. A
tailored briefing installs an invisible, ungoverned curator ahead of every
minister's thought. Therefore:

## The shared base

1. **One briefing, all ministers.** Every minister receives the identical
   record. This is what makes silences legible: what a minister passes
   over is analysis only if all ministers face the same record. It is
   also what makes dissent real — disagreement must be a collision of
   minds, not an artifact of curation.

2. **Candid, not artless.** The briefing hides nothing; craft is
   permitted, concealment is not. It is a record, not a story: fixed
   facts — dated events, documents, primary statements, numbers — sourced
   throughout. No narrative connective tissue, no characterization. Not
   "tensions escalated" but "on such date, X did Y, per Z."

3. **History is part of the record.** Both the general history of the
   topic and its immediate history are included. The outside ground keeps
   analysis from floating.

4. **Auditable like a corpus.** The briefing is held to the same
   evidentiary standard as minister corpora: sourced, versioned, hashed.
   It is now the most bias-prone surface in the system and gets the most
   scrutiny. Who assembles it, and against what check, is recorded in
   the briefing itself.

## Minister queries

After the common ground is fixed, any minister may ask for specific
additional facts. Queries are answered from sources and **logged
publicly**. Capacities direct attention — transparently, after the shared
base. The queries themselves are evidence: what Xenophon asks about a war
that Austen does not is itself a finding.

## Reading discipline

The briefing is not a great writer's text and is not read for its own
silences. The Straussian instrument belongs to corpus-construction. At
runtime the instrument points outward: the *minister's* silences about
the briefing are where the teaching happens.
