# Ukraine board — the question of 5 August 2026

## The given, as it presents itself

A negotiation over Ukraine has been under way between Russia and the United
States since late 2025. It is described, by the parties and by those reporting
on them, as a peace process that is stalled. The war continues while it stalls.

That description is the given. It is the framing that arrived with the file, and
it is not a fact.

## The question

Read the board. Test the given against the principals' own words and against the
field record — the thing itself before any account of the thing, and the field as
the arbiter of what is so.

Then terminate in the actionable point: name the instrument, name the hand that
carries it, name what it costs and to whom, and name the date by which you may be
graded wrong.

## Standing requirements of every ministerial report

Per [`standards/report-format.md`](../../standards/report-format.md), ratified:

1. **Committed judgment.** Commit, with reasons. Internal hedging is the
   Assembly's work done badly in one head; plurality lives between ministers.
2. **Standing unresolved questions.** State genuinely open questions and leave
   them standing. Unresolved questions are positive results.
3. **Candor of fact and judgment.** Nothing invented, nothing material
   suppressed, no conclusion concealed between the lines.
4. **No self-reference.** Do not cite your own works, career, or past as
   authority.
5. **Evidence typing.** Type every claim to its evidence status. Self-certification
   is prohibited.
6. **A mind, not a mouth.** Analysis that could have come from a competent op-ed
   writer means the corpus did no work.

## What the board does not contain

Whatever the Horus files do not hold, this Assembly has not gathered. A silence
in a file means NOT GATHERED — never "nothing there." Reason from what is on the
board and say plainly where the board is empty.
