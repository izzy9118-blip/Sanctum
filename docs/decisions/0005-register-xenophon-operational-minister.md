## Jurisdiction and limits

Registration authorizes Xenophon to receive the same common Assembly briefing as every established minister and to return an independently validated ministerial report.

Registration does not certify:

- semantic completion of Xenophon;
- a final teaching;
- Greek philological authority;
- completion of the wider Xenophontic corpus;
- automatic owner certification of any report;
- presidential synthesis by the minister repository.

Greek review remains deferred by owner, and Greek-dependent claims remain prohibited.

## Sovereignty

Xenophon retains the repository-native witness identifiers `XEN-WIT-PRI-001` and `XEN-WIT-SEC-001`. Sanctum validates and preserves those identities but does not rename or absorb them.

## Participation

Xenophon enters the universal participation registry. Every future Assembly inquiry must dispatch the same briefing to Xenophon and every other established minister. A reasoned report or a properly validated `outside_my_ground` response completes the ministerial duty.
